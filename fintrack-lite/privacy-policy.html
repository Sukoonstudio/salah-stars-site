FinTrack Lite Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

FinTrack Lite is provided by AxilaSoft. This policy explains how FinTrack Lite handles information.

Information handled by the app

FinTrack Lite lets you enter account names, opening balances, categories, transaction amounts, dates, descriptions and references. This information is stored in the app database on your device.

Data collection and sharing

FinTrack Lite does not transmit your financial records to the developer or third parties. It does not include advertising, analytics, user accounts or cloud synchronization. The app does not sell personal information.

Backups and exports

You may choose to create a JSON backup or CSV export using Android's system file picker. You choose where these files are stored and whether they are shared. Files stored outside the app are controlled by you and by the storage provider you select.

Security and retention

Android system cloud backup is disabled for FinTrack Lite. App records remain on the device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app database, but it does not remove backup or export files that you saved elsewhere.

Children's privacy

FinTrack Lite is a general personal finance utility intended for adults and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app behavior or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@axilasoft.com.
