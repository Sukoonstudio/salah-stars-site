Practice together, at your own pace

Salah Stars helps families track daily prayers, Quran, habits and motivational Stars on a shared device. Family records stay on the device. No Salah Stars cloud account or family sync is required.

Parents arrange every reward

Children can claim a parent-chosen reward when they have enough available Stars. Parents receive a local in-app notice and arrange the reward themselves. Salah Stars does not offer, supply, fund or deliver gifts or rewards. Stars have no cash value.

Support and privacy

Questions? Email axilasoft@gmail.com. Please do not send child names or worship histories when reporting a bug.

Core family features work offline. The optional Star Supporter badge uses Google Play purchases. Read the full privacy policy for storage, deletion and purchase details.
