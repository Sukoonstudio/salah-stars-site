Salah Stars Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

Salah Stars is provided by Sukoon Store. Privacy and support contact: tranquility.aistudio@gmail.com.

Family data stays on your device

Parent display names, child names/nicknames, avatars, prayer and habit records, Stars, reward claims and parent notifications are stored locally on your device. No Salah Stars online account is required. We do not upload family or worship records, use Firebase, or operate cloud family storage or sync. There are no ads, developer analytics or developer crash reporting.

Optional Google Play purchases

The optional Star Supporter badge is processed by Google Play and requires Google services and connectivity. Google and its billing SDK dependencies may process purchase information, device information and diagnostics under their own privacy practices. We do not receive payment-card details. The app stores local badge ownership; Google maintains purchase records for processing and restoration. The badge does not grant Stars, gifts, rewards or additional app features. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Parent PIN and recovery

Parent configuration and daily submission use a local four-digit PIN. After three incorrect attempts, PIN entry is paused for 30 minutes. Parents can instead reset the PIN using the device authentication supported by their device. Authentication is handled by the operating system; Salah Stars does not access biometric templates. Recovery changes the PIN, not family records. On a shared device, device authentication may accept another enrolled user's biometric. If device verification is unavailable, wait for the cooldown and use the existing PIN.

Rewards and gifts are arranged by parents

Salah Stars does not offer, supply, fund, sell or deliver any reward or gift. Parents choose and arrange all family rewards or gifts themselves. Stars are motivational points, have no cash value, cannot be purchased or transferred, and are not redeemable with Salah Stars or its developer. A child with enough available Stars can confirm a claim without a Parent PIN. The app deducts Stars and records an in-app notification with the child's name, reward and claim date, asking the parent to arrange the reward. This is a local family record, not an order or delivery promise.

Retention, deletion and device changes

Family records remain locally until app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Closing the local profile does not delete records or create a separate protected family account. To delete all family data on Android, open Settings > Apps > Salah Stars > Storage > Clear storage (wording varies by device), or uninstall the app. Browser users must clear this site's stored data. This deletes local family data and PIN settings; it does not delete Google Play purchase records. Google manages those records under its own policies. Salah Stars cannot retrieve or remotely delete records held only on your device. No cloud restore or family backup service is provided; device loss, clearing storage or uninstalling can permanently erase family progress. Android app backup is disabled. Local storage uses the device/application security boundary; the Parent PIN is a simple family control, not database encryption.

Children and parent guidance

Parents set up and guide use of the app. Use nicknames if preferred. Family profiles are visible to people using the same local app. No child email, phone number, location, contacts, camera or microphone access is needed. Local notifications are shown inside the app; we do not send them to a parent remotely. Parents remain responsible for appropriate goals and fulfilling rewards.

Contact and changes

For questions about privacy or app behavior, email tranquility.aistudio@gmail.com. Future changes will be reflected in this policy and its effective date.
